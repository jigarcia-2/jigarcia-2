With 10+ years in real hospital and fertility care settings, I know what it means when a number changes in a dataset — it's a patient, a decision, a life. That context is what I bring to every analytical pipeline, report and visualization I build.

Formally trained in Bioinformatics and Data Science, I build reproducible pipelines that translate complex healthcare datasets into clear, actionable insights for stakeholders — because data only matters when people can understand it and act on it.

I invite you to explore my projects, which reflect my way of interpreting numbers within their proper healthcare and clinical context.
